title: How we compare with GeoLite2 and DB-IP Lite date: 2026-11-21 summary: A new page compares the accuracy and terms of openipdata, GeoLite2 and DB-IP Lite, measured on the same RIPE Atlas devices. ---

The page Free GeoLite2 alternative compares our database with GeoLite2 and DB-IP Lite. It has two parts: the terms of the free databases, and their accuracy.

Terms

The table lists licence, sign-up and update rhythm. Ours is CC0, needs no sign-up and is rebuilt twice a day. GeoLite2 comes under the MaxMind EULA with a licence key, and DB-IP Lite is CC BY and monthly. IP2Location LITE and IPinfo Lite are in the table too. The page does not call ours a substitute for any of them; it says only that the layout is the same.

Accuracy

We measure on RIPE Atlas devices with a known location. Each device is looked up in every database, and we count three things:

  • Have a city: the share of addresses with a city in the record.
  • Right country: the country matches the device.
  • Right city: the city is within 50 km of the device.

All three databases are scored on the same devices. The numbers come from the stamp inside each build of our file, so the page updates itself when we publish a new build. The device count is shown on the page.

Guessed cities

Our share with a city includes guessed cities. Their city_source starts with 8 (80-asn-main to 89-country-near), so you can leave them out. A guess is weaker than a city from a geofeed, and the file says which is which.
